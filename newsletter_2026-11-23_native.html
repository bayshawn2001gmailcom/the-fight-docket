The Future at 205 Just Arrived, and His Teammate Holds the Belt. Navajo Stirling knocked out Jiri Prochazka at 4:58 of the second round on Nov. 21 at ABHA Arena in Doha. Two seconds before the horn, Stirling slipped a Prochazka punch and came back over the top with a right hook that put the former champion out cold. Referee Marc Goddard waved it off after two follow-up shots. Stirling is now 12-0 in MMA and 7-0 in the UFC. Prochazka, 34, falls to 32-7-1. It was the main event of UFC Fight Night 294, the UFC's second event in Qatar, and it was the only finish on a five-fight main card that produced eight decisions across 12 bouts. Stirling did not get lucky. He won the first round, stayed patient behind leg kicks, and outlanded Prochazka 48-23 in significant strikes. Prochazka, who entered ranked No. 3 at light heavyweight and had never been outclassed quite like this, looked confused by Stirling's patience and paid for it the moment he opened up. Stirling, 29, from New Zealand, took a $100,000 Performance of the Night bonus for the finish. He is now the most dangerous fighter in the division who does not hold a belt. Here is the problem. The light heavyweight championship is held by Carlos Ulberg, Stirling's teammate at City Kickboxing in Auckland. The UFC's next title fight at 205 pounds is either teammate against teammate, or the hottest fighter in the sport sits and waits while the division stalls. Stirling has now knocked out a former champion in the main event. There is no other fight for him that means anything. Ankalaev is a name, but he is not the champion, and a win over him does not settle anything. The title is the only fight that matters, and the man holding it trains in the same gym. The rest of the card told the same kind of story: the old guard losing ground. Kevin Vallejos, 24, beat former bantamweight champion Aljamain Sterling by unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 29-28) in the co-main event, stuffing Sterling's takedowns for three rounds. Vallejos is now 19-1 and 5-0 in the UFC, and he spent his post-fight interview calling out featherweight champion Alexander Volkanovski, then told UFC executive Hunter Campbell cageside that he is the next champion. He also posted that if Volkanovski moves up to lightweight, he will fight Jean Silva for the vacant title. Silva holds a win over Vallejos from the Contender Series, which makes that the better fight anyway. Some fans argued Sterling won the decision. The judges did not. Dan Hooker beat Brian Ortega by unanimous decision (30-27 on all three cards) in the feature bout, snapping a three-fight skid for his first win since August 2024. Ortega, a two-time title challenger, took his gloves off in the cage afterward and said, "I think this is the end." He retires at 16-6 after three straight losses. Kyoji Horiguchi outworked Asu Almabayev for a unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 29-28) at flyweight. Ikram Aliskerov swept Jared Cannonier 30-27 on all three cards. Amir Albazi submitted Alessandro Costa with a rear-naked choke at 4:34 of Round 3 for his first win in more than three years, even after losing a point in Round 2 for grabbing the fence. Diyar Nurgozhay stopped Alik Lorenz with a head kick at 0:24 of Round 2, and John Mitchell knocked out Shoaib Yousaf with a knee at 3:50 of Round 1. Albazi, Nurgozhay and Mitchell joined Stirling on the $100,000 bonus list. No Fight of the Night was awarded. [MMA Junkie](https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/story/sports/ufc/2026/11/22/ufc-fight-night-294-post-event-facts-dan-hooker-ends-losing-skid/92878413007/) · [MMA Junkie](https://mmajunkie.usatoday.com/story/sports/ufc/2026/11/21/ufc-qatar-results-kyoji-horiguchi-outworks-asu-almabayev/92897612007/) **Why it matters** Navajo Stirling is the future of the light heavyweight division, and the future is now. He did not edge a former champion on points. He knocked him cold with two seconds left in the round, in a main event, after winning every minute of the fight. The UFC spent years waiting for a new star at 205 pounds. It has one. The only question is whether the UFC will book the fight that matters. Stirling vs. Ulberg is teammate against teammate, and gyms hate that fight. But titles are the job. Fighters change gyms, sit out, and take bad fights to avoid teammates all the time, and every time it happens the division gets worse. Book it. And for Prochazka, the fall is real: at 34, he is no longer a title threat. He is a gatekeeper now, and the division just found the man who walks through the gate. **What to watch** - Stirling vs. Ulberg: will the UFC book teammates for the light heavyweight title? - Vallejos vs. Volkanovski, or Vallejos vs. Silva for a vacant belt if Volkanovski moves up? - Harrison's decision: still unmade. The NY Post argues there is no obvious next opponent at 135 pounds and floats a Nunes rematch at featherweight. - Price vs. Shields: the biggest women's fight that can be made in 2027. Will it happen? - Fury vs. Joshua: Cardiff Council approved the licence variation on Nov. 25.
