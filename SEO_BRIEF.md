## Content Strategy

### Core Content Types

**Newsletter Issues**
- Published weekly on thefightdocket.com
- Each issue = major SEO asset (long-form content, fighter names, fight data)
- Archive pages rank for "[Fighter name] fight news" and "[Date] boxing results"

**Blog/News Posts**
- Standalone articles covering:
  - Fight previews (before major cards)
  - Detailed fight breakdowns (with analysis)
  - Fighter profiles (career history, stats)
  - Industry news (promotions, rule changes, rankings)
  - Historical pieces (great fights, legendary rivalries)

**Social Content** (for drive back to site)
- Teasers linking to full newsletter
- Quote cards and breakdown clips
- Poll posts driving engagement

## SEO-Specific Newsletter Tips

1. **Use descriptive headlines** — Include fighter names and date: "Canelo vs. Terence Crawford Analysis: Who Wins the Undisputed Title Fight?"
2. **Optimize metadata** — Meta title and description should mention the main fight/topic
3. **Internal linking** — Link to archived fighter profiles and past fight breakdowns
4. **Keyword usage** — Naturally mention "boxing", "MMA", fighter names, date-based keywords
5. **Structured data** — Consider NewsArticle or SportsTeam schema for newsletter issues

## Special Rules

- **Be accurate with fight data** — Dates, records, rankings must be correct
- **Respect IP rights** — No unauthorized fighter photos; use original graphics or licensed content
- **Balanced coverage** — Don't favor one promotion (UFC, PBC, etc.)
- **Update fighter stats regularly** — Keep win-loss records and rankings current
- **Source attribution** — Link to original news sources, credit fighters' social media
