The 154-Pound Division Still Will Not Fight Him. Sebastian Fundora knocked out Ermal Hadribeaj at 2:59 of Round 12 on Oct. 17 at The Chelsea at The Cosmopolitan in Las Vegas. It was Fundora's fourth defense of the WBC super welterweight title. It moved him to 25-1-1 with 17 knockouts. And it changed nothing about the division he is supposed to rule. The finish came with one second left in the fight. Hadribeaj, the unbeaten mandatory challenger at 23-0-1, had hurt Fundora in Round 2 and kept coming for 12 rounds. Fundora outlanded him 202-144, with 56 body shots, and was ahead 98-92, 97-93 and 97-93 when the end came. Hadribeaj lost two points for holding, in Rounds 3 and 11, and referee Thomas Taylor waved it off after Hadribeaj beat the count. The punch was ruled legal because Fundora started it before the bell. [The Sporting Tribune](https://www.thesportingtribune.com/2026/10/17/fundora-knocks-out-hadribeaj-round-12) · [British Boxing News](https://britishboxingnews.co.uk/blogs/fight-of-the-year-2026-the-contenders-so-far/) Fundora won the vacant WBC title from Tim Tszyu by split decision on March 30, 2024. He stopped Keith Thurman in the sixth round on March 28, 2026. He has not lost since Brian Mendoza stopped him in April 2023. At 6 feet 5 1/2 inches, he is the tallest world champion in boxing, fighting at 154 pounds. The win over Hadribeaj was the fourth installment of "The Fight," the monthly series on TNT Sports and DAZN that has drawn 7.3 million total viewers through three events. The night also lost its co-main event when Brandon Figueroa fell ill after missing weight. The problem is what comes next. Fundora wants the winner of Jaron Ennis vs. Josh Kelly. Ennis holds the WBA and WBO super welterweight titles. That fight is anticipated for December 2026 or January 2027 and is not scheduled. The other names at 154, Ryan Garcia, Vergil Ortiz Jr., Bakhram Murtazaliev, Charles Conwell and Xander Zayas, are all fighting elsewhere or waiting. Fundora has the belt, the height, the punch output and the television audience. What he does not have is a dance partner. That is the gap in this division. The WBC title is supposed to be the top of the sport at 154 pounds. Right now it is a belt Fundora defends against mandatories while the money fights happen somewhere else. Ennis holds two belts and is fighting Kelly. Ortiz is the name everyone mentions and no one books. Garcia is a pay-per-view attraction who does not need Fundora. The sanctioning bodies and the promoters have built a 154-pound division where the champion is the least connected man in it. **Why it matters** Sebastian Fundora is the best super welterweight in the world, and the division is built to keep him from proving it. A fourth defense against a mandatory does not move a career. A unification does. Until Ennis or Ortiz signs, Fundora is a champion without a stage, and the WBC belt is a dead end. The promoters who control the other belts have no reason to risk them against a 6-foot-5 volume puncher who landed 202 punches in a fight. That is the business problem, and it is not Fundora's to solve. **What to watch** - Ennis vs. Kelly: does it get scheduled for December or January, and does the winner call out Fundora? - Fundora's next fight: does he get a date in early 2027, and against whom? - Gabriela Fundora defends her undisputed women's flyweight title Nov. 20 in Indio. The Garcia-Benn Super Fight on Sept. 12 drew a sold-out crowd of nearly 19,000 and an $8 million-plus gate. The roster is now over 100 fighters under contract, with media deals through Skydance globally except the UK and Ireland, where Sky Sports carries it. [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/05/tko-group-tko-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/) **Sela pays Benn's $15 million, not TKO.** Conor Benn signed with Zuffa Boxing for one fight, and the reported $15 million purse is covered by Sela, the Saudi financial backer led by Turki Alalshikh, not by TKO. TKO President Mark Shapiro said on the earnings call that TKO signed Benn for just one fight and that the reported purse is not TKO going out of pocket. That is the same $15 million figure Bob Arum cited for Shakur Stevenson's split with Zuffa Boxing, and it reframes who is funding Zuffa's biggest checks. [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/05/tko-group-tko-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/) **WWE is the soft spot.** WWE revenue dipped 1% to $399.3 million in the quarter. Live events and hospitality fell 30% on three fewer Premium Live Events and weaker SummerSlam sales. That is the one weak line in a quarter where TKO raised guidance for the second straight time. [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/05/tko-group-tko-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/) **Shapiro: TKO will cancel Middle East events if danger escalates.** TKO President Mark Shapiro told staff at an Oct. 28 town hall that TKO "will not hesitate" to cancel Middle East events if there is "danger or a further escalation," adding "damned be our budget." The U.S. State Department holds Saudi Arabia at a Level 3 "Reconsider travel" advisory. WWE moved its Crown Jewel venue on Nov. 7, and WWE reportedly offered $3,000 in hazard pay to crew making the trip. Zuffa Boxing 13 is set for Riyadh on Nov. 14, and a UFC Fight Night follows in Riyadh on Dec. 5. On the earnings call, Shapiro called the cancellation risk "extremely minimal." [POST Wrestling](https://www.postwrestling.com/2026/10/30/tko-president-mark-shapiro-tries-to-balance-safety-and-business-in-messaging-to-staff-ahead-of-wwe-ufc-events-in-saudi-arabia/) **Vanhouter could land on the Fury-Joshua undercard.** Da'Mazion Vanhouter (14-0, 11 KOs) stopped Ivan Balaz by TKO in Round 1 (2:04) at Zuffa Boxing 12, his fourth knockout win under Zuffa and his 10th first-round knockout. Dana White said he might find space for "Mazi" on the Tyson Fury vs. Anthony Joshua undercard in Cardiff on Dec. 11. Vanhouter is the only Zuffa roster member with four KO wins. Suarez is 13-1 in MMA and 10-1 in the UFC. The strawweight champion is Mackenzie Dern (17-5 MMA, 12-5 UFC).
