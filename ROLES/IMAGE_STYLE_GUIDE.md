# The Fight Docket — Visual Bible
### Image Style Guide for AI-Generated Assets

> **Every image generated for The Fight Docket must be held against this document before use. If it conflicts with any rule below, regenerate.**

---

## 1. Core Principle: Contextual Lock

**Images must be semantically tethered to the article they accompany. No exceptions.**

Before generating an image, identify the specific subject of the piece:

| Article Topic | Acceptable Imagery |
|---|---|
| Fighter contract dispute | MMA gloves resting on legal documents, contract closeups, attorneys in a gym context |
| Weight cut / health | Fighter on a scale, sweat-soaked training gear, medical instruments ringside |
| Fight card announcement | Empty arena with dramatic lighting, ring canvas at low angle, spotlights |
| Regulatory / commission news | Governing body signage, official scorecards, press conference staging |
| Training camp feature | Gym environment, focus mitts, sweat and chalk, candid mid-drill moments |
| Post-fight analysis | Ring canvas damage, corner cutmen, post-fight presser ambiance |

**Never generate:** A generic fighter silhouette for a legal story. A trophy for a retirement piece. A crowd shot for a medical investigation. Context is non-negotiable.

---

## 2. Aesthetic Standard

**Reference: HBO 24/7 documentaries. Ring Magazine editorial photography. ESPN The Magazine long-form features.**

- Photojournalistic realism — images should feel like they were shot by a credentialed ringside photographer
- Gritty and textured — sweat, chalk dust, worn leather, blood on canvas are authentic details
- Professional sports atmosphere — this is the upper tier of combat sports media, not local gym content
- Human and intimate — prioritize emotion, tension, and story over spectacle

**The question to ask:** Could this image run on the cover of a major sports publication? If yes, proceed.

---

## 3. Lighting

- **High contrast** — deep blacks, sharp highlights, no flat or even lighting
- **Cinematic and dramatic** — motivated light sources: arena spots, locker room bulbs, press conference flash
- **Shadow as a design element** — partial silhouettes, rim lighting, and hard shadows are encouraged
- **Color temperature** — cool blues and steely grays for serious/legal stories; amber and warm tungsten for human-interest or retrospective pieces

---

## 4. Color Palette

- **Neutrals first:** Blacks, charcoal, slate gray, off-white, aged canvas tan
- **Accent sparingly:** Deep red (blood, corner stools), cold blue (arena fill light), gold (championship context only)
- **Saturation:** Low to moderate. Desaturated near-monochrome is preferred over vivid color
- **Never:** Neon, oversaturated gradients, or anything that reads as "AI art" — no purple skies, no glowing energy effects, no hyper-real skin tones

---

## 5. Exclusions — Hard No List

These elements will immediately disqualify an image from publication:

- Cartoons, illustrations, or stylized art
- 3D renders or CGI-looking compositions
- Generic stock photo aesthetics (clean studio backgrounds, posed smiles)
- Vibrant, saturated "AI default" color palettes
- Fantasy or sci-fi elements of any kind
- Fighter faces (likeness/identity issues — show gloves, feet, torsos, backs, gear)
- Logos or brand marks of real organizations (UFC, PFL, Bellator, etc.)
- Text or watermarks embedded in the image

---

## 6. Composition Guidelines

- **Rule of thirds** — subject matter should not be dead-centered unless intentionally dramatic
- **Close crops preferred** — detail over wide shot; a glove filling the frame beats a full-body pose
- **Environmental storytelling** — the background should reinforce the story (empty gym = solitude; full arena = stakes)
- **Avoid posed symmetry** — authentic sports photography is asymmetric and kinetic

---

## 7. Prompt Construction Template

When building a FLUX prompt, always structure it as:

```
[Photorealistic / documentary photograph of] [SUBJECT LOCKED TO ARTICLE] [environmental context],
[lighting description: high contrast / cinematic / dramatic shadows],
[mood: gritty / tense / solemn / electric],
[camera feel: 35mm / telephoto / ringside angle],
[color treatment: desaturated / cold blue tones / warm tungsten],
[exclusions: no text, no logos, no faces, no cartoons, no vibrant colors]
```

**Example (legal/regulatory story):**
```
Photorealistic documentary photograph of worn MMA gloves resting on a legal contract and pen,
dim lawyer's office, single overhead tungsten lamp casting hard shadows,
gritty and tense mood, 50mm lens shallow depth of field,
desaturated warm tones, no text overlays, no logos, no bright colors
```

---

## 8. Final Check Before Every Generation

- [ ] Is the subject directly tied to the article topic?
- [ ] Does the lighting feel cinematic and high-contrast?
- [ ] Is the palette muted and grounded — no vibrant "AI colors"?
- [ ] Are all hard-exclusion elements avoided?
- [ ] Does the composition feel like editorial photojournalism?
- [ ] Could this run in Ring Magazine or ESPN The Magazine?

If all six boxes check — generate. If not — revise the prompt first.
